# Notice: please read before you use iSuite

## Use at your own risk

iSuite is provided as it is, with no warranty of any kind. It may have mistakes. It may read a name or number wrongly, miss a consent tick, or write a draft you would not choose. Always check what it shows you.

This is a tool for keeping notes and drafting messages. It is not financial advice, legal advice or compliance advice. Nothing it writes is a recommendation of any product.

## You are responsible for your clients' data

When you run iSuite, you decide what personal data goes into it, and you are responsible for it. That includes the names and phone numbers you store, and the consent you record.

- Get a person's consent before you contact them, and record it honestly.
- Follow the rules on unwanted calls and messages where you live. In Singapore, that includes the Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) and its Do Not Call rules. Other countries have their own laws.
- iSuite does not check the Do Not Call register for you.
- iSuite never sends messages. You send each message yourself, so you are the sender and you are responsible for it.
- Keep your computer secure. Your contacts, your bot token and your API key are stored on it.

## Your data goes to an outside AI service

To read a photo, and to write a draft, iSuite sends text and photos to Anthropic's API. Anthropic's servers are outside Singapore. Anything you send in a photo or message may be processed there, under Anthropic's own terms and privacy policy. Read them, and decide if that is acceptable for your clients' information.

Your messages also pass through Telegram.

## Do not enter ID or policy numbers

Please do not type or photograph national ID numbers, passport numbers or policy numbers. iSuite tries to remove them before it stores or sends anything, but it cannot catch every way they can be written. If one gets through, it may be stored on your computer and sent to the AI service.

## Check your own company's rules

If you work for a company, agency or licensed institution, check its rules before you use this with client data. Some firms do not allow client details in outside tools or apps. It is your job to find out and to follow them.
